## Fixed
- Some games kept showing Update even right after updating. They were being checked against another platform's version. Now each game is checked against the version that actually installs on your computer. Thanks to the player who told us.
- Decomp Buddy starts with far less work. Box art now loads as you scroll to it, not all 4,600 covers at once, and some covers are no longer shrunk again on every start. In our tests, start-up went from about 46 seconds of computer work to about 4, and memory from over 2 GB to under 1 GB.
- With Keep Running In The Background on, closing the window now just hides it, so opening it again from the tray is instant instead of a whole new start.
- Choosing an accent color in Settings → Make It Yours now changes every button, not just the main one. Most buttons used to stay pink. Thanks to the player who told us.

## New
- Move To Another Folder, in each installed game's More… menu: moves the whole game, saves inside it included, to any folder, even on another drive. It stays in Installed Games.
- Settings → Open On: start the app on Installed Games instead of Browse Games.
- Every game card now has Project Page and What's New links. They're in each installed game's More… menu too.
- Settings → Shortcut Folder: choose where Desktop Shortcut puts a game's icon, instead of always the desktop.
- Settings → Keep Old Versions: keep the last 2 (the default), just the last one, or none to save space. Your saves are still backed up before every update.
